02 — POSE

ILLUSTRATIVE

Pose Estimation

A pose model places body keypoints on every frame: head, shoulders, hips, knees, ankles. Each point carries a confidence score, and the raw signals are smoothed before anything is measured.

03 — SEGMENT

ILLUSTRATIVE

Turn Segmentation

A skier's center of mass swings side to side down the fall line. The pipeline tracks that lateral path and splits the run into individual turns, with no manual tagging.

06 — HARDWARE

CONCEPT

Boot Pod

A camera sees the body, but it cannot feel the ski. A small motion-sensor pod on each boot will record what the video misses, synced to the footage. Two mount positions are under test: the cuff power strap and the lower shell.
