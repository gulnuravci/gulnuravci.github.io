The problem

Learning to fly is expensive, and most of the feedback after a lesson is the instructor's memory of the flight. A student hears “your turns were a bit sloppy” but cannot see which turns, by how much, or whether they are getting better.

1The gap

Glass cockpits record every flight. The trainers students rent do not.

Newer airplanes can log flight data. The older trainers that flight schools rent out, the ones students can afford to fly, have no such instrumentation. Postflight closes that gap with a low-cost box that works in any airplane.

How it works

01   In the cockpit

Set it on the glare shield. Fly the lesson.

Press one button and fly. The box records how the airplane moves, its altitude, heading and GPS position, and the other aircraft nearby. It needs nothing from the airplane: no power, no wiring, no modification.

02   After landing

Debrief on the ramp.

Your phone joins the box's own Wi-Fi, copies the flight and grades it against the FAA's training standards. Did you hold altitude within 100 feet? Were your turns coordinated? Did you level off cleanly? No internet at the airport required.

03   Over time

See yourself get better.

Flights go to your account when the phone has a connection. Track whether your coordination, altitude control and landings improve across weeks of training. Instructors can follow all of their students.

Why I am building it

I am a student pilot. My debriefs were short, because my instructor had to get to the next student. Stress made me forget parts of the flight. Postflight is the debrief I wanted after every lesson.

What it is not

Not an electronic flight bag

Planning, charts and weather already belong to ForeFlight and Garmin Pilot. Postflight works after the flight, not before it.

Not a live traffic display

Traffic is debrief context: who was in the pattern with you. Nothing competes for the pilot's attention in flight.

Not a certified flight recorder

It is a coaching tool, at a price and setup an individual student or a small flight school can adopt.
